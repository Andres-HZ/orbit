# Orbit — AI Powered “What Should We Do Today?” Mobile App

## Project Vision
Orbit is a modern AI-powered mobile application that helps people decide what to do based on their current context, mood, budget, available time, weather, location, and social preferences.

The goal is to eliminate decision fatigue and instantly generate personalized plans, activities, and experiences for individuals, couples, families, or groups.

Orbit should feel:
- modern
- minimalistic
- intelligent
- social
- visually beautiful
- fast and addictive to use

The app must prioritize:
1. Excellent mobile UX/UI
2. AI-driven recommendations
3. Simplicity and speed
4. Personalization over time

---

# Main Concept

Users open Orbit and answer simple contextual questions like:
- How much time do you have?
- What is your mood?
- What is your budget?
- Are you alone or with friends?
- Indoor or outdoor?
- Calm or active plans?
- Food-related or experience-related?
- Do you want something spontaneous?

Orbit then generates intelligent plans using:
- weather
- location
- time
- user preferences
- trending events
- past activity behavior
- AI recommendation logic

# Core Features (MVP)

## 1. Smart Plan Generator
Generate personalized activity recommendations based on:
- location
- weather
- budget
- available time
- energy level
- mood
- group size

Example:
Input:
- Couple
- Rainy weather
- Low budget
- Medellín
- 3 free hours

Output:
- Cozy coffee shop
- Indoor activity
- Nearby restaurant
- Movie option
- Suggested route

---

## 2. “Surprise Me” Mode
One-tap AI-generated spontaneous plans.

The app should:
- instantly create a full plan
- include estimated costs
- estimated duration
- nearby locations
- optimized activity order

This feature should feel magical and addictive.

---

## 3. Group Planning Mode
Users can invite friends to:

Orbit should find the best common plan for the group.

The recommendation engine becomes smarter with usage.
